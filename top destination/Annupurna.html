This is North Annapurna Base Camp (old base camp) Trek


Annapurna North Base Camp is reached by a newer trail from Beni in Myagdi that takes you to the northern side of Annapurna I, away from the crowded Sanctuary trail. At Bhusket Mela (3,551 m), the new route joins the older trail from Lete in Mustang. Past that point the forest gives way to open, rugged terrain with views of Annapurna I, Tilicho Peak and the Nilgiri massif. Futfute Waterfall and Panchakunda Lake near the base camp add to the appeal.

Trekking Details

Province: Gandaki Province

Estimated Cost = NRs 15,000 to NRs 20,000

Estimated Days = 3 days 3 nights

Best Time to Trek = March to May and September to November

Difficulty Level = Moderate

  1. Bus from Kathmandu (Gangabu Bus Park) to Beni (Estimated cost="1800")
  2. Jeep from Beni to Humkhola (Estimated cost="1750")
  3. Trekking Route(Day 1)
    1. Humkhola to Guras Danda
    2. Guras Danda to PhutPhute
    3. PhutPhute to Sandhikhara
    4. Sandhikhara to Gufaphat
    5. Gufaphat to Bhusket Mela
    Trekking Route(Day 2)
    1. Bhusket Mela to Panchakunda Lake
    2. Panchakunda Lake to Annapurna North Base Camp

Recommendation : This trek is suitable for those who are looking for a less crowded trekking experience in the Annapurna region. The trail offers stunning views of the Annapurna massif and is a great option for those who want to explore the northern side of Annapurna I. And it is highly recoomended for travellers to book the hotels in advance
