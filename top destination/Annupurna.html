This is Annapurna Base Camp Trek via Ghandruk


A classic Himalayan trek from the Gurung village of Ghandruk, through bamboo and rhododendron forest, to the foot of Annapurna at 4,130 m. You get close views of Annapurna South, Hiunchuli and Machhapuchhre (Fishtail).
