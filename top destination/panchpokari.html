This is Dhorpatan Trek


Dhorpatan Trek is a less crowded alternative to the popular Annapurna Circuit. It offers stunning views of the Dhorpatan massif and is a great option for those who want to explore the northern side of the Annapurna region.

Trekking Details

Province: Gandaki Province

Estimated Cost = NRs 8000 to NRs12000

Estimated Days = 3 days 3 nights

Best Time to Trek = Sept-Nov and March-May

Difficulty Level = Hard

  1. Day 1: Kathmandu to Chimti to Tuppi Dada to Rato Mato

    • Bus fare: Rs. 650 (the bus leaves from Jorpati).
    • Lunch at Chimti: Rs. 350
    • Chimti to Tuppi Dada: moderate uphill, about 3-3.5 hours.
    • Tea break at Tuppi Dada: Rs. 50
    • Tuppi Dada to Rato Mato: about 2 hours on a gradual trail with forest sections.
    • Overnight at Rato Mato (accommodation + dinner of local dal bhat): Rs. 800

  2. Day 2: Rato Mato to Nosyampati to Panch Pokhari

    • Trail: forest trail with gradual climbs.
    • Breakfast at Rato Mato (chana, anda, and chiya): Rs. 150
    • Rato Mato to Nosyampati: about 3 hours.
    • Lunch at Nosyampati (local dal bhat): Rs. 350-400
    • Total stop time: about 1 hour (lunch + rest).
    • Nosyampati to Lauribina to Panch Pokhari: about 3-3.5 hours. This is the steepest part of the trek.
    • Overnight at Panch Pokhari (accommodation + dinner): Rs. 1,000 in the hall (shared space) or Rs. 1,200 in a room.
    • Note: Red Bull costs Rs. 200-350, depending on the altitude.

  3. Day 3: Panch Pokhari to Jugal Him Shrinkhala View Point to Chimti

    • Altitude: 4,100 m
    • Wake up around 3:45 am and take a short hike (30-40 minutes) to Jugal Him Shrinkhala View Point.
    • Enjoy the sunrise and the panoramic view of the Jugal Himal range, take photos, and soak in the peaceful moment.
    • Tea at the view point: Rs. 50-100
    • Breakfast at Panch Pokhari (chana, anda, and chiya): Rs. 150-250
    • Panch Pokhari to Nosyampati: 1.5-2 hours. The trail is a steep descent, so take it slow.
    • Lunch at Nosyampati: Rs. 350-400
    • Nosyampati to Tuppi Dada: about 2 hours.
    • Snacks at Tuppi Dada (noodles and tea): Rs. 150
    • Final stretch, Tuppi Dada to Chimti: 2-2.5 hours.
    • Overnight at Chimti (accommodation + dinner): Rs. 600

  4. Day 4: Chimti to Kathmandu

    • Breakfast at Chimti (chiya and cookies): Rs. 100
    • Bus fare: Rs. 650

Recommendation : October or April is the single best month to go for dry trails, mild weather, and the clearest mountain views.
