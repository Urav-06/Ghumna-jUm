This is Mansalu Circuit Trek


Mansalu Circuit Trek is a less crowded alternative to the popular Annapurna Circuit. It offers stunning views of the Mansalu massif and is a great option for those who want to explore the northern side of the Annapurna region.

Trekking Details

Province: Gandaki Province

Estimated Cost = NRs 25,000 to NRs 35,000

Estimated Days = 11 days 11 nights

Best Time to Trek = Sept-Nov and March-May

Difficulty Level = Hard

  1. Day 1: Kathmandu (Gongabu Bus Park) to Machhakhola

    • Take a bus from Gongabu Bus Park to Machhakhola.
    • Stay the night in Machhakhola.

  2. Day 2: Machhakhola to Jagat

    • Stay at Jagat.
    • Room: Rs. 400
    • Dinner (khana): Rs. 350
    • Breakfast (1 egg and tarkari): Rs. 250
    • Tip: food prices are per person, but 2-3 people can share one room.

  3. Day 3: Jagat to Deng

    • Stay at Deng.
    • Room: Rs. 400
    • Veg khana: Rs. 450
    • Breakfast: depends on what you order.

  4. Day 4: Deng to Namrung

    • Package offered: Rs. 1,500 per person including breakfast.
    • Prices differ from hotel to hotel, so negotiate.

  5. Day 5: Namrung to Shyala

    • Stay at Shyala.
    • Room: Rs. 500
    • Khana: Rs. 450
    • Breakfast: prices go up a little the higher you climb.
    • Shyala is the best place to stay for the view of Manaslu and the sunrise.

  6. Day 6: Shyala to Samagaun

    • Have breakfast in Shyala, then visit Pungen Gumpa on the way to Samagaun.
    • Stay at Samagaun.

  7. Day 7: Rest and acclimatization day at Samagaun

    • Gain some altitude and let your body adjust.
    • Optional: hike to Manaslu Base Camp (about 8 hours up and down) and come back to Samagaun.
    • Room: Rs. 500
    • Khana: Rs. 450
    • Breakfast: Rs. 300 (negotiable)

  8. Day 8: Samagaun to Dharmasala

    • If you skipped Manaslu Base Camp, stay at Samdo (the stop before Dharmasala) for acclimatization.
    • Dharmasala is a bit expensive, so negotiate.
    • Room: Rs. 1,000
    • Khana: Rs. 1,100
    • Hot water (1 litre): Rs. 900 at the hotel, but there is a water tap nearby where it's much cheaper.

  9. Day 9: Dharmasala to Bhimtang (cross Larkya La Pass)

    • Have a good breakfast at Dharmasala and carry energy bars and food.
    • There is no tea house on the way, so carry 1 thermos of warm water, another water bottle, and 2-3 jeevanjal.
    • Drink water frequently on this day.

  10. Day 10: Bhimtang to Besisahar

    • Walk about 6-7 hours to Tilche, then take a jeep to Besisahar.
    • If you have a group, reserve the jeep early while coming down from Bhimtang.

  11. Day 11: Besisahar to home

    • Head back to Kathmandu or Pokhara, or wherever you're from.
    • Kathmandu: easy to find a hiace from Besisahar Bus Park.
    • Pokhara: there can be a 3-4 hour gap between vehicles, so check ahead.
    • Another option is to take a vehicle to Dumre and continue from there.

Recommendation : This trek is suitable for those who are looking for a less crowded trekking experience in the Annapurna region. The trail offers stunning views of the Annapurna massif and is a great option for those who want to explore the northern side of Annapurna I. And it is highly recoomended for travellers to book the hotels in advance
